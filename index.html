Positionality Declaration · Your One Methodological Choice

Declare whether the AI should interpret from inside your domain expertise or from an external perspective. This has real methodological consequences that must be declared upfront (Braun & Clarke, 2022).

🏠
Insider Positionality
Interpret from within the researcher's domain. Leverages contextual knowledge, shared understanding, and disciplinary familiarity. The AI "thinks with" the field.
✓ Rich contextual interpretation
✓ Domain-sensitive coding
✓ Recognizes field-specific patterns
⚠ Risk of inherited blind spots
⚠ May normalize what should be questioned
🔭
Outsider Positionality
Interpret from an external, fresh-eyes perspective. Questions assumptions, surfaces taken-for-granted knowledge, and provides analytical distance. The AI "thinks about" the field.
✓ Fresh perspective on assumptions
✓ Surfaces hidden norms
✓ Questions what insiders accept
⚠ May miss contextual nuance
⚠ Risk of misreading domain language
